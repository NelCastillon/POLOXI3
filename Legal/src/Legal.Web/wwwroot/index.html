WHY DECISION INTELLIGENCE

Designed around the failure modes of high-stakes legal analysis.

Relevance is not enough.

A source can be highly relevant yet have little effect on the decision. Judz.ai prioritizes evidence by its ability to discriminate between competing outcomes.

Confidence is not readiness.

A leading candidate does not mean the matter is resolved. Readiness considers essential requirements, verification, unresolved high-impact issues and adversarial testing.

A fact is not evidence.

Evidence items and fact propositions remain distinct, preserving provenance and making assumptions, authenticity and support visible.

A source is not authority.

Retrieved material must pass identity, proposition and authority checks before it can support a decision path.

Winning once is not enough.

The strongest losing position is reconstructed before convergence, reducing the risk that the system merely reinforces its first plausible theory.

Every decision has a flip point.

Judz.ai identifies the smallest material change capable of altering the current outcome, giving attorneys a concrete view of residual risk.
