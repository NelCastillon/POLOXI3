AgencyBinder / Security & AI Governance

Security, tenant isolation, and governed intelligence

AgencyBinder combines tenant-aware access, identity governance, traceable business activity, protected document operations, and policy-controlled AI assistance.

Control extends from data access to AI-assisted decisions.

Tenant context, authorization policies, review requirements, safety controls, and audit evidence follow business records, documents, workflows, search, recommendations, and AI execution.

Tenant-Aware Access

Business data, configuration, documents, reports, and intelligence remain scoped to the authorized tenant context.

Identity Governance

Roles, permissions, groups, scopes, MFA, SSO, access reviews, and segregation-of-duties controls manage access.

Audit Evidence

Entity changes, user actions, approvals, security events, document access, exports, and AI decisions are traceable.

Governed AI

Feature policies control models, limits, confidence, safety, grounding, evaluation, and mandatory human review.

Layered controls

Governance across the complete platform.

Security and accountability are applied as shared platform capabilities rather than isolated module settings.
