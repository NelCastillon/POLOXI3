FAQ

Pricing questions answered.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes. Change your plan anytime with no downtime. You'll be prorated based on your usage. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

Do you charge per policy or per user?

AgencyBinder charges per platform + per user. There are no per-policy fees. You control your costs by choosing the right plan and user count.

What's included in "per user" pricing?

Each user seat includes access to all features in your plan tier, unlimited data storage per tenant, and full support at your plan level.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Foundation and Professional plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get access to everything in that tier.

How does the annual discount work?

Annual plans are prepaid and save you 15% vs. monthly pricing. You can still upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time; the annual commitment is to service, not to a specific tier.

Do you have an on-premise or self-hosted option?

AgencyBinder is a cloud-only SaaS platform. We do offer dedicated cloud environments and custom deployment options for Platform-tier customers. Contact our sales team to discuss.

What happens if I go over my user count?

We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can add users anytime. The cost will be prorated and added to your next invoice.

Do you offer discounts for annual commitments or volume?

Yes. Multi-year and volume discounts are available for Enterprise and Platform plans. Contact sales@agencybinder.com to discuss your requirements.

What support do I get at each tier?

Foundation: Email support. Professional: Priority email. Enterprise: Phone + email. Platform: Dedicated success manager + all above.

Can I add users later if I start small?

Absolutely. Start with just a few users and add more as you grow. You'll be charged prorated pricing for each billing cycle.
