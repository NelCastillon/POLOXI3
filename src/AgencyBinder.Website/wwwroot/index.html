FAQ

Common buyer questions.

Is AgencyBinder built for multi-tenant SaaS?

Yes. The product is designed around tenant provisioning, subscription controls, feature flags, TenantId inheritance, tenant-scoped reporting, and tenant-safe AI insights.

Can every agency have different workflows?

Yes. Tenant Admins can configure agency-specific carriers, markets, templates, workflows, rules, branding, branches, users, and permissions.

Does the system support policy servicing?

Yes. The policy workspace supports endorsements, certificates, documents, service tasks, billing visibility, commissions, renewals, and claims context.

Can this integrate with carriers or external systems?

Yes. The architecture supports integration APIs for carrier submissions, rating, document storage, email, accounting, CRM, and reporting tools.
