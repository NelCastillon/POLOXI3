# Finance Module - Quick Reference Guide

## Access Finance Workflows

Navigate to **Billing & Accounting** → **Finance** in the sidebar, or access directly via:

### Financial Core Operations
1. **GL Accounts** - `/finance/glaccounts`
   - View and manage general ledger accounts
   - Filter by account code, name, or type

2. **Journal Entries** - `/finance/journalentries`
   - Track all journal entries
   - View entry dates, descriptions, and status

3. **Journal Entry Lines** - `/finance/journal-entry-lines`
   - Detailed line items for journal entries
   - View debits and credits by account

### Bank Operations
4. **Bank Reconciliation** - `/finance/bank-reconciliation`
   - Reconcile bank accounts
   - Track variances and reconciliation status

### Payable Management
5. **Vendors** - `/finance/vendors`
   - Manage vendor master data
   - View vendor details and status

6. **AP Invoices** - `/finance/ap-invoices`
   - Track vendor invoices
   - Monitor invoice dates and amounts

7. **AP Invoice Lines** - `/finance/ap-invoice-lines`
   - View invoice line details
   - Track account allocations

8. **AP Payments** - `/finance/ap-payments`
   - Record vendor payments
   - Track payment dates and amounts

### Period Management
9. **Accounting Periods** - `/finance/accounting-periods`
   - Define fiscal periods
   - Manage period dates and status

10. **Period Close** - `/finance/period-close`
    - Track period closing activities
    - View close dates and who closed periods

### Revenue Management
11. **Deferred Revenue** - `/finance/deferred-revenue`
    - Manage deferred revenue contracts
    - Track recognized vs. deferred amounts

12. **Def. Rev. Recognition** - `/finance/deferred-revenue-recognition`
    - Record revenue recognition transactions
    - Track recognition dates and amounts

### Special Transactions
13. **Bad Debt** - `/finance/bad-debt`
    - Record bad debt write-offs
    - Track write-off dates and amounts

14. **Cash Receipts** - `/finance/cash-receipts`
    - Record customer payments
    - Track receipt dates and amounts

### Reporting
15. **Trial Balance** - `/finance/trial-balance`
    - Generate trial balance reports
    - View debit and credit balances by account

## Common Actions

### Search
- Use the search box in each workflow to filter results by keyword
- Examples:
  - Search "1000" in GL Accounts to find accounts starting with 1000
  - Search "ACME" in Vendors to find ACME vendors

### Refresh Data
- Click the **Refresh** button (<i class="bi bi-arrow-clockwise"></i>) to reload data

### Navigate Pages
- Use the pagination controls to browse multiple pages
- Each page shows 12 items

### Sort Data
- Click any column header to sort by that column
- Click again to reverse sort direction

## Keyboard Shortcuts
- `Esc` - Close dropdowns or dialogs
- `Tab` - Navigate between fields
- `Enter` - Submit search or form

## Tips & Tricks
- **Favorites**: Star any menu item to add to your Favorites section at the top
- **Recent**: Accessed workflows appear in the Recent section
- **Mobile**: Use the menu icon in mobile view to access Finance workflows
- **Search Navigation**: Use the global search bar to quickly find any workflow

## Related Modules
- **Billing** - View AR invoices and payments (from Finance Cash Receipts)
- **Accounts** - Customer master data (for receivables)
- **Analytics** - Financial Analytics dashboard
- **Reports** - Financial reports and statements
- **Commissions** - Commission calculations based on finance data
